The Open Healthcare Group

A smarter way for better healthcare

Since the late 1990s the Open Healthcare Group has published open standards and open-source software for health records. We now also publish policy proposals on how American healthcare is paid for. The group is the work of Jonathan A. Borden, MD, a practicing neurosurgeon.

New · Policy proposal · November 2026

A fair and coherent way to reduce drug prices

Medicare bluntly negotiates the drug prices for Part D and gives private plans a competitive domestic supply

Physicians and hospitals are already paid within structured systems built on Medicare's relative values and negotiated contracts. Prescription drugs should be too. The proposal offers manufacturers a large national market at a defensible international price. If a manufacturer refuses, qualified domestic producers could be authorized to supply the medicine, with compensation for patent use. It asks Congress to specify the program and have the savings independently scored.
